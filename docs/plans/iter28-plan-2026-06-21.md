## Part 1 — Behavioral fixes (drive-assist), priority order

**P1 — `poly-distrust` regression (THE severity fix).** iter-23 K1 / iter-25 M4 made `poly-distrust`
ignore the polyline / suppress `lateral-eject` whenever the native `IS_POINT_ON_ROAD` reads False (to
avoid false ejects on rural dirt where the native is *wrongly* False). **But when the native is
*correctly* False and the car is genuinely stuck, it suppresses the very eject that would trigger
recovery.** Proven: during the beach wedge `poly-distrust` fired continuously F6716→F7645+ at
`nativeOff=True lat=−13 speed=0.0`, while `lateral-eject` fired **once all session**. Fix at the K1 eject
gate (~GTA11Y.cs:10097): add a *genuinely-stuck-off-road* override that forces the eject / arms recovery
when `!IsPositionOnRoad AND speed < ~2 m/s sustained > ~2 s AND |lat| large` — i.e. poly-distrust may
veto a *moving* car's eject, never a *stopped, off-road, persistently-stuck* one. Pair with making
stuck-recovery actually arm in this state (see P-note below).

**P2 — Polyline node-snap stability.** The severe lateral hits (#2 junction, #8 Z-level, #3 U-turn) are
single-frame path discontinuities. (a) `StabilizePolyline` lateral-jump hold (~GTA11Y.cs:20329) is gated
`prevDist<3 && IsPositionOnRoad` — extend it to also hold when off-road and to reject a fresh lead node
whose Z differs > ~3 m from the car (the #8 "snapped to the road below" case). (b) The low-speed recovery
flip-reject (iter-17 CC / iter-24 R2) is speed-gated and let a 144.7° U-turn target through at low speed
(#3) — reject `>FLIP_ANGLE` recovery targets regardless of speed.

**P3 — Point-blank brake latch + ground-graze (the deferred iter-28 B-1/B-2, now evidenced).** At the
collisions `EVENT brake-floor-release` fired (`brakeDist 0.3–0.99 < min 1.0`) and the prop was
`STATIC-REJECTED reason=below-bumper` — the brake was disarmed and the obstacle dismissed one frame
before impact. B-1: when `closestBrakeDistance < BRAKE_MIN_USEFUL_DIST` with a valid recent closing
threat and speed > ~2, hold brake/handbrake instead of disarming (~:10806/10813). B-2: when a static hit
is rejected as below-bumper but a known prop/vehicle entity sits within ~1 m at blocking height, treat it
as real (corroboration, not blanket widening — ~:9434-9463).

**KEEP lane-offset (iter-26 B1) DARK-LAUNCHED — do NOT enable yet.** This log is the calibration evidence
and it says a *fixed* right offset is wrong: it would only half-correct #1 (+5.45, needs ~2× the 2.65
offset) and is wrong-signed/harmful for #5 (−4.7 m **left**, divided road where offset is correctly 0).
Lane-centering needs a *signed, per-side* model (push toward the correct lane, not always right) before
`laneOffsetPolyline` flips on. Keep gathering `poly-offset` data.

**Defer:** AEB-on-stationary (B-3) — not prominent this session.
