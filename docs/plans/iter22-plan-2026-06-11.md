Three root causes, all readable straight from the poly-lat term telemetry:

1. **OFF-ROAD-PARALLEL IS INVISIBLE TO THE MODE MACHINE.** F4861: car 15.6 m off the path,
   eRate≈0 (tracking PARALLEL to the road, 15 m beside it), cross saturated at the new 0.35 cap,
   speed 13 m/s, mode still LaneKeeping. The lane-fail gate (iter-17 Fix 1b) requires lateral
   departure (>2.5 m) **AND** look-ahead skew >30° — a parallel departure has skew≈0, so
   recovery never engages, and the I1-capped controller (correct for oscillation!) can only
   crawl back at 0.35 rad. The iter-17 AND-gate fixed junction phantoms but created this hole.
2. **CURVATURE FEED-FORWARD FIGHTS THE CORRECTION WHEN OFF-PATH.** F6005 (the sea run): car
   +4.2 m right of path needing LEFT steer (cross=-0.229) while ff=+0.202 anticipated a
   right-bend the car wasn't even on — net steer ≈ 0 at 24 m/s; sixty frames later lat=+23.9
   and the car was in the water. Anticipating path curvature is only meaningful when ON the
   path.
3. **NO WATER SENSING.** Casts are map-geometry only; the Alamo Sea reads as open space.

iter-22 = three small fixes + the thrice-carried lane offset. All in GTA\GTA11Y.cs (+MapDb for

## J1 (P0) — Lateral-only lane-fail: a hard departure ejects to recovery regardless of skew

In ProcessSteeringAssist, at the skew-fail gate block (search `lookaheadSkewFail`): add a
parallel HARD-LATERAL path that does not require skew:
- New consts near `LANEFAIL_LATERAL_GATE_M` (search it): `LANEFAIL_LATERAL_HARD_M = 6.0f`
  (≈ off the pavement on any road; normal tracking <1 m, weave extremes were 5-12 m),
  `LANEFAIL_LATERAL_HARD_FRAMES = 6` (same streak style as LANEKEEP_SKEW_FAIL_FRAMES).
- `bool hardLateralFail = Math.Abs(lastLaneLateralError) > LANEFAIL_LATERAL_HARD_M;` maintain
  its own streak counter (new field `hardLateralFailStreak`); when streak ≥ frames →
  `laneKeepOk = false` (same effect as the skew path) + throttled
  `EVENT lateral-eject: lat= speed=` (~1/s).
- Reset the streak when |lat| < 4 m (hysteresis) and on vehicle change (with the other streak
  resets, search `skewFailureStreak = 0`).
- Junction-phantom safety: the iter-17 dogleg false-skew problem was ANGULAR; a genuine 6 m+
  LATERAL offset cannot be a path-regen artifact (path regen inserts segments near the car —
  lateral distance to the polyline stays small). GPS-sourced doglegs keep their existing gate.
- Effect: recovery (FindBestRecoveryNode → GetAlignmentRecoverySteer + recovery braking) engages
  within ~0.3 s of a real departure instead of never, and its braking sheds the speed that made
  departures unrecoverable.

## J2 (P0) — Feed-forward proximity scaling (stop fighting the return)

In ComputeStanleySteer where ffTerm is computed (search `iter-21 I3`): scale by path proximity
before the clamp:
`ffTerm *= Math.Max(0f, 1f - Math.Abs(lateralError) / STANLEY_FF_FADE_M);` with new const
`STANLEY_FF_FADE_M = 3.5f` — full anticipation when tracking tightly, zero when >3.5 m off the
line (you are not on that curve). Keep the ±0.25 clamp. The ff= telemetry field already exists
for verification.

## J3 (P1) — Water-ahead guard (the Alamo Sea class)

New helper in the drive-assist region: probe 2 points ahead along the VELOCITY direction at
~1.0 s and ~2.0 s of travel (clamped 8–50 m): for each, `GET_WATER_HEIGHT`
(hash 0xF6829842C06AE524, returns bool + out height; called via Function.Call with raw hash and
OutputArgument per house style). Water detected when the native returns true AND
`waterZ > probeGroundZ... simpler and robust: when it returns true and `waterZ >
playerPos.Z - 2f` (the car is near/above water level — a bridge deck high above water stays
clear because the deck route's waterZ is far below the car; tune in log).
- Run it inside ProcessSteeringAssist once per scan when `vehicleSpeed > 8f` (cheap: 2 native
  calls); set `waterAheadDetected` + distance.
- Response: treat like the skew-emergency — uncapped `liveBrakeTarget = 1.0` in
  ApplyCachedSteeringInputs while detected and speed > 4 (new block next to the iter-16 FIX 6
  skew-emergency, search `SKEW_EMERGENCY_ANGLE`), gated `!graceActive && !alignmentEngageReverse`;
  Speech.Critical("Water ahead!") with 5 s cooldown; throttled `EVENT water-ahead: dist= wz=`.
  Do NOT auto-steer (a blind-player surprise swerve is worse than a hard stop).
- Amphibious/boat vehicles: ProcessSteeringAssist already returns early for boats; also skip
  when `playerVeh.IsAmphibious` if the property exists in SHVDN 3.6 — otherwise skip this guard
  (rare case, acceptable).
