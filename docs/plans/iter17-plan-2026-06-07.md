## 4. The fixes (ranked by impact-vs-risk)

Fixes **1–3 are the cure** (stop the phantom from ever triggering recovery); **4 + backstops are containment** so any future phantom cannot become a 20 s stuck. Conventions: `roadHeadingDelta` is degrees (sign `−headingError`); `lastLaneLateralError` is meters (+ = path to vehicle's right). New constants go in the constants block (~:276-908) near siblings.

### FIX 1 — Look-ahead heading for FAILURE detection (windowed minimum). [HIGHEST IMPACT / LOW-MED RISK]
**The Stanley *control* steer keeps using the closest segment (correct for control). Only the failure-detection angle gets look-ahead.**

**Where:** `ComputeStanleySteer` :17909-17980 (add, don't replace, the control path); gates :8774 & :8788.

**Change:** After the existing closest-segment computation, compute a **windowed-minimum** heading error: walk the polyline forward from the closest point and sample `|heading error|` at arc-length steps `0, STEP, 2·STEP, …` out to `LANEFAIL_LOOKAHEAD_MAX`; take the **minimum-magnitude** sample (carry its signed value) into a new field `lastLookaheadHeadingDelta`. If the polyline ends before MAX, include the final segment tangent (that's the real road). Drive the fail gates off `lastLookaheadHeadingDelta`.

> **AGENT-3 CORRECTION (critical — Agent-2's single-point look-ahead was wrong):** Agent-2 proposed `L = clamp(0.45·speed, 5, 15)`. At F2839 (speed 11.4) that yields **5.1 m**, which lands *on* the **8.63 m** dogleg → look-ahead skew still ~85° → **would NOT catch the logged case.** A dogleg segment can be as long as one GPS sample step (`PATH_GPS_SAMPLE_STEP=12 m`). The window MUST clear that. Use a **fixed window with min-over-samples**, not a single speed-scaled point — so a single perpendicular segment is always straddled, while a *genuine* sideways car (every sample ~90°) still fails.

**New constants:**
```
private const float LANEFAIL_LOOKAHEAD_MAX  = 20.0f; // m; > one GPS sample step (12 m) so a single dogleg is cleared
private const float LANEFAIL_LOOKAHEAD_STEP =  4.0f; // m; ~5 samples across the window
```
At F2840: samples at 0/4/8 m sit on the 8.6 m dogleg (~85°); samples at 12/16/20 m sit on the south leg (~14°). **min = ~14° < 30°** ⇒ no failure ⇒ the car drives straight through the junction and the entire downstream stuck never happens. A real in-place spin-out on a straight road reads ~90° at *every* sample ⇒ min ~90° ⇒ fails correctly. A legitimate upcoming sharp turn has an aligned pre-turn sample ⇒ low min ⇒ no false "failure" (control still steers through the bend).

**Fix 1b (apply at gates):** at :8774 and :8788 replace `Math.Abs(roadHeadingDelta)` with `Math.Abs(lastLookaheadHeadingDelta)`. (Note `LANEKEEP_SKEW_SEVERE_ANGLE` is a *local* const at :8787 — edit that line in place.) Keep `roadHeadingDelta` unchanged for control + logging continuity. The 55° severe-eject uses the look-ahead value **without** a lateral precondition (so a genuine in-place spinout still ejects); the 30° gate adds the lateral precondition (Fix 2).

**New EVENT (next-log proof):** when the gate *would* have fired on `roadHeadingDelta` but the windowed look-ahead is benign, emit once:
`EVENT lookahead-suppress: closeSkew=<F1> aheadSkew=<F1> lat=<F2> speed=<F2>`. Also append `aheadSkew=<F1>` to the per-frame `road:` line for full auditability.

**Lane departure should require lateral offset, not heading alone (LDW literature; see §5).** A car centered in its lane at a junction has small `lastLaneLateralError`.

**Where:** skew-fail gate :8774 (the 30° path only).

## 5. Research backing (Agent-2, citable)
- **Heading error from a look-ahead point, not the closest point**, is the standard cure for closest-point (Stanley) controllers' sensitivity to waypoint/junction discontinuities; pure-pursuit is *defined* against a look-ahead point for exactly this reason. (dingyan89 "Three Methods of Vehicle Lateral Control"; Roboquest "Stanley Controller".)
- **Look-ahead is bounded** `[L_min, L_max]`; we deliberately use a *fixed window with min-over-samples* rather than a single `k·v` point because the discriminator here is geometric (clear a ≤12 m dogleg), not speed. (autonomous-driving-book pure-pursuit; arXiv 2409.09848.)
- **Cross-track (lateral) error is the lane-departure signal; heading error is the noisy one.** Production LDW triggers on ~0.2–0.5 m past boundary / TTLC ≈ 1 s; correct-tracking CTE ≈ 0.5–0.9 m — so a departure gate at ~2.5 m avoids nuisance trips. (Bosch LDW; Sage robustness-of-LDW study; arXiv 2409.09848.) This is exactly the F2839 signature: **small lateral (on the road) + huge heading (perpendicular dogleg)** = the phantom.
