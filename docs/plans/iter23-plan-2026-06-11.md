# iter-23 plan — un-poison the lateral eject (J1 regression), polyline ground-truthing

## Verdict from driveassist-2026-06-11-192838.log (iter-22, 50,857 frames / ~20 min, ~60% off-road terrain)

The tester is right: iter-22 made off-roading WORSE, and the mechanism is identified.
**~70% of the 224 new `lateral-eject` events are PHANTOM** (7/10 sampled episodes): the
node-graph polyline intermittently snaps to a PARALLEL road / dirt trail / perpendicular
segment 6–20 m away (countryside graph is full of them), so |lateral error| > 6 m while the
car is physically fine on the real road. Before J1 this polyline noise was harmless (the
iter-17 lateral+skew AND-gate filtered it); J1 removed the skew requirement and converted
latent polyline-quality noise into ~224 recovery entries → recovery braking + high-authority
steer toward the WRONG alignment (recovery-search occasionally perpBand=True, headingDelta
up to −80°) → the increased off-road excursions, 149 mode-changes, 18 stuck-arms, 11 graces,
and 5 assist-suspends (each stranding the player 3.4–11.9 s). On-road tracking BETWEEN
episodes is still good (lat < 5 m; the I1/I2 damping holds). 6 auto-collisions all prop/
terrain (no vehicle-on-vehicle; worst −54.9 body in the western mountains); total body damage
−55 net. Water guard: 2/2 events were FALSE positives on shoreline roads (~(1327,3697),
road ~0.5–1.7 m above lake level; velocity probe crossed the shoreline on a curve).

Root principle for iter-23: **the polyline is a HINT, not ground truth — never punish the car
for the polyline's mistakes.** The game provides ground truth cheaply: `IS_POINT_ON_ROAD`
(already wrapped as `IsPositionOnRoad`, GTA11Y.cs ~search `IS_POINT_ON_ROAD`).

## K1 (P0) — Ground-truth gate on the lateral eject

At the J1 block (search `EVENT lateral-eject`): when the streak threshold is met, call
`IsPositionOnRoad(playerVeh.Position)` (cache 1 result per scan):
- Car OFF road → eject as today (genuine departure; this is the case J1 was built for).
- Car ON road → do NOT eject; emit throttled `EVENT poly-distrust: lat= headingDelta=` and
  set a `polylineDistrustUntilTicks` (~1.5 s). Expected effect: kills the ~157 phantom ejects
  while keeping the Alamo-Sea-class real departures.

## K2 (P0) — Polyline lateral-jump hysteresis (extend StabilizePolyline)

StabilizePolyline (search `POLYLINE_FLIP_ANGLE`) currently holds the previous polyline only on
DIRECTION flips. Add a LATERAL criterion: if the fresh polyline's closest-segment lateral
distance to the car exceeds `POLYLINE_LATERAL_JUMP_M = 5f` while the PREVIOUS polyline's was
< 3 m AND the car is on-road (`IsPositionOnRoad`), hold the previous polyline (same
POLYLINE_MAX_HOLD cap). Plus: while `polylineDistrustUntilTicks` (K1) is active, prefer the
held polyline. This attacks the snap-to-parallel-road source directly; throttled
`EVENT poly-hold: reason=lateral dist=`.

## K3 (P1) — Water-guard false-positive fix

In the J3 probe block (search `EVENT water-ahead`): alarm only when BOTH probes detect water
OR (single probe AND `!IsPositionOnRoad(probePoint)`), and tighten the height test to
`waterZ > playerPos.Z - 1f`. Shoreline roads stay quiet; a genuine drive-into-the-lake
trajectory (probe points on water, off road) still alarms + brakes.

## K4 (P1) — Suspend stranding mitigation

5 suspends stranded a blind player 3–12 s. In the resume logic (search `ASSIST_RESUME_SPEED`):
lower to `speed > 3 m/s sustained 2 s`, and add an auto-resume after `ASSIST_SUSPEND_MAX_TICKS
= 20 s` regardless of speed ("Drive assist resumed.") — suspension should be a cooldown, not
an abandonment. Keep the toggle path. NOTE: with K1/K2 the upstream eject storm collapses, so
suspends should become rare anyway.
