# iter-19: "Never lock the throttle and keep it" — three-log lockup analysis + hotfix plan

## Context — what the three iter-18 logs show

Sessions analyzed: `driveassist-2026-06-09-204938.log`, `-212048.log`, `-215409.log` (all banner-confirmed iter18).
Good news first: the new telemetry worked, the cruise quality genuinely improved, and **zero health
damage all three sessions** (the early "collisions" were frustration flags, not impacts). The fatal
complaint — "assist locked the throttle and would not release" — happened in **three different ways**:

1. **Reverse-pulse re-arm defeats the watchdog (204938).** Wedged in RecoveringToRoad, the recovery
   crawl accelerates the car toward 3.0 m/s (= `REVERSE_UTURN_SPEED`), reverse engages on the dip,
   breaks in ~3 frames, re-arms every ~0.6 s — 21+ cycles/17 s. `watchdog-release` fired 3× but
   `GetAlignmentRecoverySteer` re-set `alignmentEngageReverse` within ~1 s, and `inRecoveryMode`
   (mode != LaneKeeping) kept `DISABLE_CONTROL_ACTION` live continuously. Speech said "Assist
   driving / You have throttle" ~21× at ~0.8 s intervals.
2. **Stale scan-owned brake floor (215409 — cleanest trap).** The last scan set
   `curveBrakeRequest=0.476`; the scan then never ran again (upstream gate — no autodrive events, no
   logged errors; vehicle-exit or a swallowed scan exception are the candidates) while the apply path
   kept flooring `rampedBrake` at 0.476 → `brakeIntervening` → throttle disabled every frame → car
   braked 10.3→0 m/s and sat trapped to session end. `curveBrakeRequest` has **no expiry**; neither
   `ForceReleaseAssistHold` nor the onTick else-reset clears it; the watchdog gate doesn't cover it.
3. **Recovery can't escape, escalation unreachable (212048).** Same wedge + 11 reverse pulses; the
   watchdog DID release cleanly here, but the car was physically stuck and stuck-recovery Layer 2/3
   (pivot / wander-autodrive) never armed — **stuck-arm count = 0 in all three sessions** — because
   the no-progress arm requires speed < 0.7 m/s sustained and the assist's own pulses cycle speed
   0.5↔3.0, resetting the clock (net displacement < 2 m over 13–17 s).

Supporting findings: **ground-graze occlusion** — the swept-sphere casts (origin z+0.5, radius ≥ 1.0)
graze the road 0.7 m ahead (`STATIC-REJECTED relZ=-0.18 normalZ=1.00`; 821 rejected vs 2 accepted in
212048); closest-hit semantics mean the graze occludes real walls, so the long-range static cast is a
~1 m sensor — why the wedge geometry was never seen coming. **Speech spam** — "Braking, obstacle
ahead" fires on `brakeMag>0.5 OR |steer|>0.3`; aReq telemetry shows real braking severity (aReq ≥ 3.5)
in only ~14% of applies. **Telemetry verdicts**: shape-test status is definitively 2=Ready
(s1 never occurs; pre-iter-18 builds discarded every swept-sphere result → cancel old plan item A2,
same-frame reads are valid); poly-lat confirms the polyline is road-centerline (car sits +2–3 m right;
lane-offset calibration banked for a later iteration).

Goal of iter-19 (hotfix-scale, ~250 lines, all in `GTA\GTA11Y.cs`): the player must ALWAYS get control
back, escalation must actually escalate, and the two P1 quality fixes (cast geometry, speech). Lane
offset is **deferred** (not hotfix-shaped; sign error risk = steering into oncoming).

Anchors below verified against the current uncommitted iter-18 tree. Implementation order
F1→F2→F4→F3→F5→F6→banner; build (`dotnet build` Release) after each; each independently revertable.
No new natives (SHVDN 3.6 constraint satisfied).

## F1 — Scan-staleness dead-man (kills Mechanism 2, any cause)

- New: `lastScanCompletedTicks` stamped as the LAST statement of `ProcessSteeringAssist` (after
  `PushDriveAssistSnapshot` ~:9767) so early-returns/exceptions leave it stale; const
  `SCAN_STALE_TICKS = 6000000` (600 ms ≈ 12+ missed scans).
- Top of `ApplyCachedSteeringInputs` (~:11646): if stale → zero `curveBrakeRequest`,
  `recoveryBrakeRequest`, `cachedBrakeMagnitude`, cached threat positions, `cachedSteerCorrection`,
  `cachedHandbrakeMagnitude`, `accThrottleOut/accBrakeOut`; drop `emergencyBrakeActive`, `brakeArmed`,
  `alignmentEngageReverse`. Let `rampedBrakeInput` decay via the normal down-ramp (no lurch).
  Throttled `EVENT scan-stale-release: ageMs= rampedBrake=`.
- `bool inputDisablesAllowed = !scanStale` (F2 ANDs in `!graceActive`) gates ALL
  `DISABLE_CONTROL_ACTION` sites (~:11892, :11895-11899, :11906-11907) and the throttle-lockout
  block (~:11988).
- Also clear `curveBrakeRequest` + `cachedHandbrakeMagnitude` in `ForceReleaseAssistHold` (~:11251)
  and `curveBrakeRequest`/`recoveryBrakeRequest`/`alignmentEngageReverse` in the onTick else-reset
  branch (~:3795-3806). Extend the watchdog `assistHoldingStopped` composite (~:11808) with
  `|| curveBrakeRequest > 0.3f`.
- Diagnostic for the unexplained scan stop: make the drive-assist catch (ReportSubsystemError path,
  ~:2381/:3720) also write `EVENT subsystem-error: <subsystem> <ex.GetType+message>` to driveLogger
  (throttled) so a swallowed scan exception is visible next session.
- Red-light safety: while stopped with ACC, the alive-gate keeps the scan running → stamp stays
  fresh → dead-man can't fire. It only fires in the pathological apply-without-scan state.

## F2 — Watchdog grace + suspend escalation (kills Mechanism 1)

- New fields: `assistGraceUntilTicks`, `assistSuspended`, `assistResumeSpeedOkSinceTicks`,
  `lastSeenSteeringAssistMode`; consts `WATCHDOG_GRACE_TICKS=7 s`, `WATCHDOG_REFIRE_WINDOW_TICKS=30 s`,
  `ASSIST_RESUME_SPEED=5 m/s`, `ASSIST_RESUME_HOLD_TICKS=3 s`; helper `InWatchdogGrace()`.
- `ForceReleaseAssistHold`: first fire → latch grace, announce "Assist released. You have control.",
  `EVENT watchdog-grace`. Second fire within 30 s of grace expiry → `assistSuspended=true`,
  Speech.Critical "Drive assist suspended.", `EVENT assist-suspend`.
- During grace: force `useLaneKeep = true` (one line before `DriveMode prevMode` ~:9183 — the
  LaneKeeping branch tears down recovery latches incl. `alignmentEngageReverse` for free; recovery
  re-entry blocked while failure streaks keep accumulating, so recovery resumes right after grace);
  zero `curveBrakeRequest` after its write (~:8994); in Apply, block `brakeArmed` unless live TTC
  < 0.4 s (genuine emergency still brakes via SetControlNormal, never via disables);
  `inputDisablesAllowed &= !graceActive`.
- Keep the monitor alive during grace: extend the onTick alive-gate (~:3774) with
  `|| InWatchdogGrace()` so UpdateStuckRecovery keeps running; stuck Layer-1 entry cancels grace
  (`EVENT grace-cancel: reason=stuck-arm`) — escalation outranks player-sovereignty once the player
  demonstrably can't self-escape.
- Suspension enforcement: `&& !assistSuspended` on the master gate (~:3753); resume on setting
  toggle (detect `steeringAssistMode` change) or speed > 5 m/s sustained 3 s → "Drive assist
  resumed.", `EVENT assist-resume: reason=toggle|speed`.

## F3 — Displacement-based stuck arm (makes Layer 2/3 reachable; Mechanism 3)

- In `UpdateStuckRecovery` after the existing no-progress block (~:10942): rolling anchor
  `dispArmAnchorPos/dispArmSinceTicks`; gate `assistCommanding = inRecovery || alignmentEngageReverse
  || pivotEscapeActive` OR `fwdThrottle > 0.25` (already reads GET_DISABLED_CONTROL_NORMAL so intent
  registers while locked). Net planar move < `STUCK_DISP_MIN_M=2.5` over `STUCK_DISP_WINDOW=6 s`
  → `noProgressArm = true` regardless of instantaneous speed.
- `EVENT stuck-arm` gains `arming=displacement` + `dispMs=`; reset anchor in `ResetStuckRecovery()`.
- Red-light safety: hands-off → gate false; throttle-into-stopped-car for 6 s → Layer 1 (upright +
  rear-vetoed reverse) is a defensible response. Complementary to squareOnStall (which keeps the
  blocked+throttle instant case); deliberately does NOT require `blockedAhead` (occlusion bug showed
  threat caches can be empty exactly when wedged).
- Resulting ladder in a wedge: watchdog 4 s → grace (player tries) → displacement arm ~6 s →
  pivot/Layer-2 → Layer-3 wander autodrive. Deterministic, ends in escalation instead of a loop.

## F4 — Takeover announce hygiene

- Replace the `throttleTakeoverAnnounced` block (~:11917-11937) with a debounce: state must be stable

  Mechanism-1-style pulsing now reads as ONE continuous takeover: one spoken pair total.

## F5 — Ground-graze cast geometry fix (the occlusion bug)

- Lift the swept-sphere origin so the sphere BOTTOM sits at vehicle-origin **+0.25 m** — below the
  static-cast relZ acceptance floor (0.30, ~:8363) so zero currently-accepted curb/step hits are
  lost, above the observed road graze (−0.18): in `PerformShapeCast` (~:12550) and
  `PerformStaticWallCast` (~:12578), after radius computation:
  `startPos.Z += Math.Max(0f, (radius + CAST_BOTTOM_CLEARANCE_M) - 0.5f);` (param is by-value; the
  5-ray fan's startPos at the call site is untouched — fan unaffected, still catches low props).
- Cap the static-cast radius 2.2 → `STATIC_CAST_RADIUS_CAP=1.4` (lane half-width + margin): off-lane
  hits were cone-rejected anyway; smaller radius needs less lift and bounds the new overhead-occlusion
  exposure. Append ` lift=` to the STATIC / STATIC-REJECTED log lines.
- Expected: STATIC-REJECTED ground spam disappears; static hits appear at 10–20+ m; walls get cached
  as brake threats BEFORE contact (the missing warning in all three wedges).
