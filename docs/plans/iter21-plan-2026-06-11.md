## I1 (P0) — Cross-track saturation + speed-scheduled gain (`ComputeStanleySteer`)

At the cross-track computation (search `float crossTrack = (float)Math.Atan2(STANLEY_K`):
- Clamp the error magnitude fed to the controller: `eEff = sign(e) * min(|e|, STANLEY_CTE_CAP_M)`
  with `STANLEY_CTE_CAP_M = 4.0f` — beyond 4 m the demanded correction stops growing (normal
  tracking error is <1 m; 10+ m errors must NOT command full lock).
- Clamp the term itself: `crossTrack = clamp(crossTrack, ±STANLEY_CROSS_MAX_RAD)` with
  `STANLEY_CROSS_MAX_RAD = 0.35f` (~20°; heading term still adds on top for genuine skew).
- Speed-schedule the gain: `kEff = STANLEY_K * (0.60f + 0.40f * min(speed / 20f, 1f))` — full
  K=1.5 at ≥20 m/s, 0.9 at crawl, directly removing the low-speed gain blow-up.
New consts near `STANLEY_K` (search `private const float STANLEY_K`).

## I2 (P0) — Lateral-error rate damping (the missing D term)

Fields: `prevStanleyLatError`, `prevStanleyLatErrorTicks` (reset to invalid on vehicle change /
teleport — hook the existing resets near `lastLaneIndex = int.MinValue`, and when the polyline
is empty). In `ComputeStanleySteer` after lateralError is computed:
`eRate = (lateralError - prevStanleyLatError) / dtSec` (guard dt 0.02-0.5 s; skip the term when
invalid). Damping: `damp = clamp(STANLEY_KD * eRate / (speed + STANLEY_SOFT), ±0.25f)` and
`steerRad = -headingError + crossTrack + damp`… NOTE SIGN: damping must OPPOSE error growth —
positive eRate (path moving right = car drifting left… verify against the existing sign
convention comments at the lateralError computation) ⇒ the term must subtract from the
correction that is already being applied. Implement as `steerRad += dampTerm` with
`dampTerm = -clamp(STANLEY_KD * eRate / (speed + STANLEY_SOFT), ±0.25f)`… **the implementer
must verify the sign empirically against the convention comment block ("Positive = path is to
the RIGHT of the vehicle") and via the I-verify telemetry below before trusting it.**
`STANLEY_KD = 0.6f` starting value (yields ~0.1 rad at 3 m/s lateral closure, 18 m/s forward).
Telemetry: extend the existing 1 Hz `EVENT poly-lat` line (search `EVENT poly-lat:`) with
` eRate=` + ` cross=` + ` damp=` (the three term values) so the next log shows phase
relationships and the sign can be audited offline.

## I3 (P1) — Curvature feed-forward (kills the high-speed drift-out on curves)

The B3 design from the standing improvement plan: at the closest segment, compute Menger
curvature κ from polyline points (a,b,c) around the look-ahead vertex (the points are already
walked in `ComputeCurveBrake`, search `CURVE_BRAKE_LOOKAHEAD_PTS`, and in the iter-17 windowed
walk inside ComputeStanleySteer): κ = 2·|cross(b−a, c−b)| / (|b−a|·|c−b|·|c−a|), signed by the
cross direction. `ff = clamp(atan(PURE_PURSUIT_WHEELBASE · κ · sign), ±0.25f)` added to
steerRad. Skip when segments shorter than 1 m or polyline <3 points. Log `ff=` on the poly-lat
line. Risk: doubled correction on sharp bends — the ±0.25 clamp + existing total steer clamp
bound it.

## I4 (P1) — H4 lane-offset polyline (unchanged spec from approved iter-20 plan; still pending)

- Per static-graph segment: `bwd>0` (two-way; node line = divider) → offset right
  `laneWidth*(0.5+laneIdx)` with laneIdx snapped from player lateral + `LANE_CHANGE_HYSTERESIS_M`
  via `lastLaneIndex` (deleted LaneCenterFromNode recipe — pointer comment, search `iter-18 B4`);
  `bwd==0` (one-way) → snap to nearest lane center `((i+0.5)-fwd*0.5)*laneWidth`. laneWidth via
  `NodeGraph.GetFlags & Freeway ? LANE_WIDTH_HIGHWAY : LANE_WIDTH_SURFACE`. Clamp |off| ≤ 5.5.
  GPS/native segments: offset 0, log-only.

  `steerPolyline` = polyline + rightPerp·offset, tapered to 0 within 10 m of vertices turning
  >25° (junction corner-cut guard). `ComputeStanleySteer` consumes `steerPolyline` when valid
  and same-count; corridor filter + `ComputeCurveBrake` keep the RAW polyline.

  settings registration — plus idToName label "Drive assist lane offset (experimental)").
