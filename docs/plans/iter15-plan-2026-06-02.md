## 3. Root-cause analysis (corrected against code + log)

### Cluster 1 — reverse into an undetected prop  *(confidence: HIGH)*
The recovery node search (`FindBestRecoveryNode`, scoring at lines 10504-10508) picked a node requiring a ~135° heading change. Stuck-recovery layer 2 then engaged **reverse** (`alignmentEngageReverse=true`, applied via `playerVeh.ForwardSpeed = -3f` at line 11910) and backed the car toward that target. A static prop ~2.6 m behind was **not seen** by the forward/side shapecasts (the nav-assist L/C/R/B casts only look forward + one rear cast), so nothing vetoed the reverse. Throttle (control 71) was disabled during the reverse (`totalTakeover` path, lines 11748-11749, plus the reverse block's own disable at 11906-11907), so the player could not override. Impact: −54.4.

The 3-frame takeover oscillation (no release hysteresis — see Cluster 2) made this worse: each re-engage re-applied −3 m/s reverse, so the car kept grinding backward into the prop instead of stopping after the first contact.

> **Note on Stage-1 "shapecast never saw it."** Confirmed for the swept-sphere casts. The mod DOES have a rear `World.Raycast` + a rear `PerformShapeCast` (lines 3360-3381), but their result (`distBehind` → `navAssistDistBehind`) is currently used only for rear-proximity beeps; it is **not** consulted before a reverse takeover. That is the gap FIX 3 closes.

### Cluster 2 — wedge trap / brake oscillation  *(confidence: HIGH on mechanism; see corrections)*
The car stopped ~1.9–2.9 m short of a small obstacle at ~(1042.2,3542.9,33.7) — note Z≈33.7 vs vehicle Z≈33.23, i.e. ~0.5 m above the chassis. Throughout the cluster the **nav-assist shapecasts read `navDist L/C/R/B=none`** and there are **no `shapecast: STATIC` or `STATIC-REJECTED` lines at all**. The threat is logged as `type=obstacle` / `brakeObstacle=obstacle` (not `wall`). This points to the **inner-fan `World.Raycast` brake/steer source** (lines 7833-8081) hitting a thin/small prop the swept sphere steps over — i.e. **a real but small, unswervable object**, NOT a pure phantom.

The 23 s is an **oscillation between two distinct brake drivers**, which is the key correction to the Stage-2 framing:

- **Phase A (cache-fed brake, `emergencyBrake=False`):** e.g. F3080 and F3270 show `rampedBrake` at 0.49–1.0 while `emergencyBrake=False`. This brake comes from `ApplyCachedSteeringInputs`: `cachedBrakeThreatPos` is ~2 m ahead, the car is ~stationary, so `CalculateTTC` → `liveTtc < 0.4` → `liveBrakeTarget = 1.0` (lines 11584-11595). The cached point keeps this brake on between scans.
- **Phase B (scan-fed emergency latch, `emergencyBrake=True`):** e.g. F3340–F3400 show `emergencyBrake=True`, `rampedBrake→1.0`. Here the live scan re-acquired the obstacle (`closestBrakeDistance` ≈ 1.9 m from the inner-fan ray), arming `emergencyBrakeActive` at line 9394.
- **The clears:** `brake-cache-early-clear: long-wedged` (F3402/3587/3774) wipes the cache and drops `emergencyBrakeActive`. Brake decays (`rampedBrake` falls), `assistActive=False` for a beat, then the next scan re-acquires the SAME ray hit, re-caches, re-arms → re-pins. ~3.1 s cycle.

Throttle is disabled during the braking phases (`throttleLockoutActive`, lines 11827-11834, fires on `emergencyBrakeActive || rampedBrakeInput > 0.05f`). Crucially, **during the post-clear gaps the mod is NOT braking and `assistActive=False`** (F3300/F3500/F4000/F4437 show `rampedBrake=0.000 emergencyBrake=False assistActive=False`), so control 71 is *not* being disabled by the mod in those windows — yet the car still does not move (`planar=0.000m`). That means for at least part of the 23 s the car is **physically wedged on the real prop and/or the player is not applying throttle**, not held by a mod brake lock. The lived "can't drive forward or backward" is the *aggregate* experience: forward is blocked by the prop, the brake re-arms whenever the player creeps toward it, and the one reverse attempt (F3281) self-cancelled in 3 frames.

> **Why `[F3281] arming=blocked`:** `squareOnStall = blockedAhead && fwdThrottle > 0.5f` (line 10901). The obstacle satisfied `blockedAhead`, but the player (blind, dead pedal) was not holding >0.5 throttle, so the square-on arm never fired. This validates Stage-2's Patch-Y observation. (Confidence: HIGH.)

## 5. Fix plan (ranked)

### FIX 1 — Stop holding a stopped car against a small confirmed obstacle (root fix for the 23-s trap)
- **Targets:** cache WRITE at **8480** (the site Stage 2 missed); early-clear block 8524-8608; emergency arm/release 9392-9412; per-frame cache brake 11584-11614.
- **Change:**
  1. On a `long-wedged` clear (8594-8607), record a short-lived **rejection locus** (the cleared XYZ + a ~4–5 s expiry timestamp). At the cache WRITE (8480) and at the emergency-arm gate (9392), if the candidate brake point is within ~2 m of an unexpired rejection locus **AND the vehicle is essentially stopped** (`vehicleSpeed < ~1 m/s`), refuse to re-cache / refuse to re-latch. This breaks the ~3.1 s re-infestation cycle.
  2. The arm gate at 9392 already requires `vehicleSpeed > 1f`, so a *stopped* car cannot newly arm `emergencyBrakeActive` — good. The leak is the **cache-fed** `liveBrakeTarget` at 11590 (`liveTtc < 0.4 → 1.0`) which holds full brake at 0 m/s. Add: when the car is stopped (`< ~0.5 m/s`) and the cached point is within the rejection window, zero `liveBrakeTarget` for that point.
- **Expected effect:** after one long-wedged clear, the brake stays released at that locus, so the player's own throttle (or the FIX-2 watchdog) can move the car instead of being re-braked every 3 s.
- **Guards / risks:** rejection must be **scoped to the locus + a short window** and keyed on `vehicleSpeed < threshold`. The moment the car moves toward any obstacle, the normal live arm (9392, gated `vehicleSpeed > 1f`) and the cache TTC must re-enable braking — a real wall must never be ignored while approaching it. Do **not** reject based on shapecast-none alone (the inner-fan ray legitimately sees this prop).
- **SHVDN native:** none new (state vars + existing arithmetic only).

### FIX 2 — Blind-player escape hatch / lockout watchdog (the literal "can't drive forward or backward")
- **Targets:** throttle-lockout 11827-11834; takeover disables 11734-11749; player-input reads 10898-10900.
- **Change:** track a "mod is holding the car stopped" timer — true only when `cachedIsFullMode && (emergencyBrakeActive || rampedBrakeInput > 0.05f || totalTakeover)` **AND** `vehicleSpeed < ~0.5 m/s`. If that condition persists and the car has moved `< ~1 m` for `> ~4 s`: force-release for a cooldown — drop `emergencyBrakeActive`, clear the brake cache, set the FIX-1 rejection locus, stop disabling control 71, and announce "You have control." Additionally, honor a **sustained** player throttle/brake input (read via `GET_DISABLED_CONTROL_NORMAL` 0,71 — already used at 10899) as an immediate takeover-abort.
- **Expected effect:** the car can never be held by the assist with throttle locked and zero motion for more than ~4 s; the player always regains a live pedal.
- **Guards / risks:** **MUST gate on the assist itself holding the car** (latched brake / takeover), NOT on a plain stop. A normal red-light/traffic stop has `emergencyBrakeActive=false` and `rampedBrakeInput≈0` (ACC comfort braking is gentle and TTC-gated), so the watchdog will not fire there and will not shove the player into traffic. Re-arm normally the instant a live obstacle re-enters the brake cone while moving.
- **SHVDN native:** none new (reuses `DISABLE_CONTROL_ACTION`, `GET_DISABLED_CONTROL_NORMAL`, `GET_CONTROL_NORMAL`).

### FIX 3 — Rear-path check before any reverse takeover (fixes the −54.4 collision)
- **Targets:** reverse entry points 11050, 11069; reverse apply 11898-11911; recovery scoring 10504-10508. Reuse the existing rear cast: `behindDir`/`PerformShapeCast` (3360-3372) or `rayBehind`/`World.Raycast` (3375-3381), whose result lands in `navAssistDistBehind` (3515).
- **Change:** before committing a reverse takeover (setting `alignmentEngageReverse=true` at 11050/11069, and before `ForwardSpeed=-3f` at 11910), require a fresh rear clearance read; if a rear hit is `< ~3 m`, suppress the reverse this frame and escalate (hold/await, or fall through to the next stuck layer). Optionally hard-reject a recovery target with `|headingDelta| > 120°` **only when** a near-rear obstacle exists — leaving the soft `flipPenalty` (10505) untouched otherwise so the "recovery always finds a node" invariant (10496) is preserved.
- **Expected effect:** the F1214–1226 reverse-into-prop loop is vetoed; the car will not back into the ~2.6 m-rear prop.
- **Guards / risks:** `navAssistDistBehind` is only refreshed when `isMovingBackwards || driveAssistEnabled` (3358) — in Full-mode drive assist that is satisfied, but confirm the value is current (not stale from a prior pose) at the decision point; if stale, issue a one-shot rear cast at the reverse-commit site. Keep the rear cast `IntersectFlags.Map` consistent with the existing call so no new native is introduced.
- **SHVDN native:** none new (reuses `World.Raycast`/`PerformShapeCast` + `IntersectFlags.Map`).

### FIX 4 — Takeover / reverse release hysteresis (no 3-frame surrender)
- **Targets:** `totalTakeover` recompute 11726-11729; `alignmentEngageReverse` set/clear sites; takeover release announce 11772-11779.
- **Change:** add an engage timestamp (e.g. `alignReverseSinceTicks`, stamped when `alignmentEngageReverse` first goes true). Require **sustained release evidence** — min ~300 ms engaged, OR proven progress (planar distance gained / obstacle cleared) — before allowing `alignmentEngageReverse` to drop. This stops the F1214→1217→1220 (and F3281→3284) churn.
- **Expected effect:** a reverse maneuver either commits long enough to be meaningful or is vetoed up front by FIX 3 — no more 3-frame on/off that both (a) re-rams the prop in cluster 1 and (b) wastes the one escape attempt in cluster 2.
- **Guards / risks:** the min-engage timer must still yield to FIX 2's watchdog and FIX 3's rear veto (safety overrides hysteresis). Cap the max engage so a stuck reverse still escalates to the next layer (the existing `STUCK_LAYER2_TICKS`/`PIVOT_MAX_TICKS` timeouts at 11053/11073 remain the upper bound).
- **SHVDN native:** none new (timestamp bookkeeping).

### FIX 5 — Patch Y gate loosening (lower priority; largely moot if 1+2 land)
- **Target:** `squareOnStall = blockedAhead && fwdThrottle > 0.5f` at 10901.
- **Change:** allow a confirmed square-on wedge to arm without requiring pre-held throttle — e.g. `blockedAhead && stoppedForWindow && (emergencyBrakeActive || rampedBrakeInput > 0.05f)` — so a blind player with a dead pedal still triggers recovery. Keep a guard against disturbing a deliberate stop (require the obstacle to be genuinely in the forward corridor, which `blockedAhead` already checks via `PlanarDist < 3.0f` + forward-dot at 10893-10897).
- **Expected effect:** the `[F3281] arming=blocked` case arms instead, giving the stuck layers a chance — but note this only helps if FIX 3/4 make the resulting reverse safe and committed.
- **Guards / risks:** loosening this re-introduces the risk Patch Y was guarding against (recovery firing during a legitimate stop). Land FIX 1+2 first; only loosen FIX 5 if the watchdog + rejection locus prove they prevent a false recovery from trapping the player. **Do not** drop the `blockedAhead` corridor check.
- **SHVDN native:** none new.

## 7. Suggested implementation order (testing branch)

1. **FIX 2 (watchdog)** — highest user-visible value and lowest risk; guarantees the blind player is never trapped with a dead pedal regardless of which brake driver is active. Lands the safety floor first.
2. **FIX 1 (rejection locus)** — removes the re-infestation cycle that the watchdog would otherwise have to keep rescuing; the two compose (FIX 1 sets the locus, FIX 2 also sets it on force-release).
3. **FIX 3 (rear-path veto)** — directly prevents the −54.4 reverse collision; independent of 1/2.
4. **FIX 4 (release hysteresis)** — pairs with FIX 3 so a vetted reverse commits instead of churning.
5. **FIX 5 (Patch Y loosening)** — last, and only if 1+2 demonstrably prevent a false recovery from trapping the player.

After each, capture a fresh `driveassist-*.log` and re-run the Stage-1/2/3 pipeline; watch specifically for: zero `AUTO-COLLISION` during reverse, no `brake-cache-early-clear: long-wedged` recurring >2× at one locus, and no stopped-with-throttle-locked interval exceeding the watchdog window.
